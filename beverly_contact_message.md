**Legal Context & AGPL Compliance:**

However, I need to address that your current use creates an AGPL v3.0 compliance issue. The Agent-MCP framework is licensed under GNU AGPL v3.0, which is a strong copyleft license with specific requirements for commercial use:

- **Section 5(c):** Derivative works must be licensed entirely under AGPL
- **Section 13:** Network use requires offering complete source code to all users
- **Section 4:** Proper copyright attribution is required

Your Beverly Knits ERP system, while built on Agent-MCP, appears to be used commercially without meeting these copyleft obligations. This creates legal risk for Beverly Knits.
